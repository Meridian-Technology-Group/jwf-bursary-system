# CR-002: Grant Tracker migration

**Statement of work under clauses 2.2 and 9.5 of the Master Services Agreement dated 1 May 2026**

## 1. What this covers

Moving the 273 live 2026-27 bursary accounts out of Symplectic Grant Tracker into the bursary system, with each assessment rebuilt by the current model from the Grant Tracker fields you have identified as trustworthy.

Your schedule of awards, scholarships, school fees and payable fees is the authority. **No family's award changes.**

---

## 2. Scope

**System changes**

- Old Palace as a school in its own right: a fee per pupil, no VAT, bursary ending at Year 11.
- The JWF-PB bursary type at Whitgift. Award types are then Trinity — JWF, TBF; Whitgift — JWF, JWF-WSP, WFA, JWF-PB.
- Migrated accounts: no applicant form, application tab disabled, everything else behaving normally.

**The accounts**

- All 273: contact register, a parent login created silently, an active bursary account, and the remaining assessment years worked out from the pupil's 2026-27 school year and shown on the parent's home page. No email is sent to any parent.
- Each assessment rebuilt from the trusted Grant Tracker fields. Your award and payable fees recorded as the confirmed figures, the rebuilt recommendation alongside them, and the difference recorded as a gap with your reason code.
- Sibling names carried into the award tab for the assessor, with sibling fees as you supply them in the report.

**Your review**

- An exceptions pack: the accounts that cannot move mechanically, plus the checks you asked for.
- An account-by-account report: Grant Tracker beside the rebuilt figures, the gap and its direction, and each account's written synopsis. This is what you sign off.
- Two full rehearsals on the test system, the first including undoing and redoing the whole migration, before anything goes near the live system.

**Documents**

- For each account, the latest year's documents and Grant Tracker's PDF of the application or annual review, checked file by file against the archive.
- An index of the earlier years so any document can be found by account reference.

---

## 3. Not included

- Years before 2026-27, and closed accounts. Options in §5.
- Grant Tracker's calculated figures, progress reports, application forms as data, email correspondence, and its finance and report sections.
- Second-parent records.
- Automated sibling linking or fee absorption.
- Changes to the assessment model.
- Any link to the billing system.
- Hosting of the earlier-year document archive.

---

## 4. Covered by your licence, at no charge

- The two new year-on-year reason codes.
- A fault in next spring's rolling-over invitations.
- Dashboard and watchlist counts not recognising locked awards.
- The gap figure on the live assessment you reported on 19 September.
